Who this is for

Psych4Everybody is for adults who never had the chance to study psychology, but have always wondered why people do what they do. We start with relationships, because that is what people ask about most: why the same arguments keep coming back, why some people bond and others drift, what actually keeps love going.

Every topic starts from a real question, explains what the research says in plain language, and tells you how confident we can be. No exams, no jargon you have to memorize, and nothing you do here is tracked.

How to use it
1

Pick a question

Choose the topic that matches what you are wondering about. You can start anywhere.

2

Read a section

Each section is short and ends with a few questions to check yourself, plus ideas to try in your own life.

3

Test the ideas

Try the flashcards and real-life scenarios. They show you what stuck, with explanations for anything you missed.

How sure can we be?

Psychology is full of popular claims. We label the evidence behind each idea so you can tell them apart.

Strongmany studies, different methods, consistent findings Mixedreal findings, but limited, conditional, or overstated Contestedpopular, but the research does not back it
